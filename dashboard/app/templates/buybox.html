Buy Box defense {{ store.name }}

What this page does

OnBuy gives the "Buy Box" (the add-to-basket button) to one seller per product. Once a day the store checks every in-stock listing; where a rival holds the box at a lower price, the store lowers its own price to just under theirs - but never under its price floor, the lowest price that still leaves your profit % after OnBuy's commission. Here you see how the last runs went, switch the daily run on or off, and change the two numbers it uses. Looking is safe. Saving the settings and a live run change real prices, so they ask for the confirmation box.

How it decides

For each in-stock listing that is not protected

1. It asks OnBuy who holds the Buy Box and at what price. Holding it: nothing to do.

2. A rival holds it at a higher price than ours: our price is already lower, so the box depends on ratings or delivery. Nothing to do.

3. A rival holds it at a lower price: it works out the floor from the sheet's cost, the shipping and the category's commission. No cost on the sheet = never touched.

4. The rival's price minus the undercut is at or above the floor: the price is lowered to that. Otherwise it is held and nothing is lowered; a price that is already under the floor goes back up.

5. A listing that was contested yesterday and no longer is goes back to the sheet's selling price.

Listings on the protected list (a content problem) are skipped. The result of every run is written to the "BuyBox" tab of the store's sheet.
