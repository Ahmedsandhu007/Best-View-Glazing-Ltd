What it checks and what each severity means

The sources, store by store

Act now = wrong or late right now: an order past its dispatch date or due today, an eBay / Amazon / backfill / nightly run that failed (a scheduled run; one you started by hand is only a look), an Amazon sync that ran out of Keepa tokens.

Look today = worth a minute: orders due within two days, a list of orders that is old or missing, a schedule that is late, a run queued for days (a GitHub ghost), failing automated tests, updates to OnBuy switched off, Amazon prices that fell by a quarter or more in one sync, a sale that ends within two days, commission tables that changed.

For your information = what last night's check did (listings zeroed for showing another product, rows deleted, prices corrected) and the listings it froze in the last week. Routine: no action unless one of them should be selling.

Orders come from each store's latest orders lookup, so press Refresh on the Orders page to bring them up to date. Everything else is read from the runs and their logs.
