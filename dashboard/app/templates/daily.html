Rows that keep failing

The nightly fixes prices, re-zeroes oversold listings and deletes dead rows one by one, and OnBuy refuses a few every night. One refusal means little - the same SKU refused night after night is a dead row that will not heal on its own. Read from the last {{ problem_nights }} nightly logs.
