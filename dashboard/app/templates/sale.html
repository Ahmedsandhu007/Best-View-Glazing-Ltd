Sale a sale % for each price range of {{ store.name }}

What this page does

Puts a time-limited sale on a store: pick price ranges, give each a sale %, set the last day and a margin floor (so no sale price leaves less than your minimum profit). Preview counts what would happen and changes nothing; Apply puts the sale live on OnBuy for customers; Cancel ends it. While a sale runs, the nightly job keeps new and re-priced listings on it until its last day.

Plan a sale

Each row is a price range of the listing's price on OnBuy, both ends included; the first row that holds a price decides. Type the sale % you want in a range, leave it empty (or 0) to keep that range out of the sale. A price that sits in no row gets no sale. {% if state and state.active %}A sale is running: applying replaces it.{% endif %}

From £ (empty = open)To £ (empty = open)Sale %

The floor keeps a sale price from leaving less than that profit on cost + shipping after OnBuy's fee. A listing with no cost on the sheet stays out.

Preview reads the whole account and shows, per range, how many listings would go on sale, how many stay out (no cost, under the floor) and the total discount. It changes nothing (a few minutes).

Apply sends the sale prices to OnBuy and saves the plan in the repository so the nightly job can keep it up to date. Customers see the sale within a minute or two.

Try it on one listing first

Puts the first matching range's sale on that one listing, waits for it to show, takes it off again and prints the timeline (about 3 minutes).
