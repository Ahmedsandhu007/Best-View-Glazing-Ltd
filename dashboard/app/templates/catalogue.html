Catalogue listings held at zero, wrong content and the dead-row purge of {{ store.name }}

What this page does

Lists the listings that are frozen (stock held at 0) because their OnBuy page shows another product, and the automatic purge of dead eBay rows. For each frozen listing decide: it is the same product worded differently (Same product), it has been fixed (Release), or it is wrong and should go (Delete). You can also freeze a listing by hand. Looking and dry runs are safe; releasing, freezing and deleting for real ask for the confirmation box.

Orphans live listings on no sheet row

What this shows

Listings that are live on OnBuy but sit on no row of the product tabs - mostly the manual and import era. They get no price fix, no stock sync and no purge: an in-stock orphan sells at whatever price it was left with, so those come first. The list comes from the latest content scan (the nightly runs the same scan). Deciding their fate is yours: the SKU box pastes straight into the Delete page's delete-by-list when a batch should go, and the CSV carries every row for a ticket or a sheet import if they should be adopted instead.
