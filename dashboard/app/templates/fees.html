Fees what OnBuy deducts on {{ store.name }}, and what the sheet assumes

What this page does

OnBuy takes a commission that depends on the product's category. The sheet prices each row with the fee it assumes; this page shows which fee model the store uses, keeps the commission tables fresh, compares what the orders really paid with the sheet's assumption (so under-charged rows get fixed) and lets you move rows to the right category. The reports are safe to run; saving the fee model, committing tables, writing overrides and relabelling ask for the confirmation box because every sync prices from them.

OnBuy's commission is a rate per category (7% consumer electronics, 13% DIY, 15% most things, 20% jewellery; some tiers have a second rate above a price threshold) plus 20% VAT on the commission, so a 15% category costs 18% of the price. In category mode the formula deducts each row's tier; in flat mode it deducts {{ model.flat | num }}% + VAT = {{ model.flat_effective | num }}% on every row. Tier mode "{{ model.tier_mode }}": {{ 'the upper rate applies to the part of the price above the threshold' if model.tier_mode == 'marginal' else 'the upper rate applies to the whole price once it passes the threshold' }}.

Save sets the store's fee variables: from the next sync every row is priced with the chosen model (category tables = each row's own tier, flat = one rate for all). It does not touch OnBuy by itself.

What OnBuy really charged

OnBuy bills the tier of ITS catalogue product's category, not the sheet's. The orders are the ground truth: each states the commission taken. A row OnBuy charges more than the sheet's tier gets a Fee % override (the sync re-prices from it and never overwrites it); a row it charges less is listed only.

Categories and their tiers

The sheet's category decides the tier in the price formula, so a row must carry the category OnBuy really uses (3D printers sit under Arts, Crafts & Sewing at 15%, not under Printers at 7%). The census shows which rows match a title pattern and how the priced rows spread over tiers and Fee % cells; the relabel moves matching rows to the right category (sheet only, backup tab first; the sync re-prices them from the new tier).
