Pricing the profit schedule of {{ store.name }}

What this page does

Every selling price on the sheet is cost + shipping + a profit % that depends on the cost (the schedule), worked out after OnBuy's commission. Here you see the schedule in force, try a price in the calculator, and change it. Preview only reports how many rows would move; Apply re-prices the sheet (old values are copied to a backup tab) and saves the schedule; Push writes the sheet's prices onto the live OnBuy listings. Only Apply and Push change anything, each behind its own confirmation box.
