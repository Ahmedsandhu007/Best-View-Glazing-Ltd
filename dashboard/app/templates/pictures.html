What this page does

Some products stay stuck on OnBuy with their pictures "loading" and a product page that does not open: OnBuy's image workers time out now and then and the pictures are never applied (their own answer - it is on their side, not our links). Two things help. 1. The ticket: paste the SKUs you found - or upload the browser scan's CSV, and only the stuck products are taken - and the dashboard builds the file OnBuy support works from (OPC, SKU, main image link, additional image links), checks that every image link opens, and drafts your reply to their agent. 2. The resubmission: OnBuy's own workaround - the same products are sent to OnBuy again with their OPC and picture links, a small test batch first. Building the ticket changes nothing on OnBuy (it only reads the sheet and OnBuy). Only "Canary" and "Resubmit the rest" change anything, for the SKUs you named only, and they ask for the confirmation box.

What the build does

  • Saves the list as cleanup/image_ticket_skus_<date>_<id>.txt in the repository. Lists of earlier days are how it knows a product was reported before.
  • Reads the sheet (a read-only run, no OnBuy): for each SKU its OPC, sync status, main picture and additional pictures; SKUs that are not on the sheet or are on it twice are listed.
  • Asks OnBuy for the OPC of SKUs the sheet has none for (a read-only lookup of the store's listings; OnBuy allows about {{ data.sweeps }} of those an hour, and the resubmissions count too).
  • Opens every picture link from this computer, as a browser would, to see that it answers with a picture. A product with a link that does not open is left out of the attachment and listed below.
  • Writes three files: the attachment for OnBuy support, an internal reference sheet (do not send) and a draft of your reply.

Nothing is sent to OnBuy support from here: you attach the file and send the reply yourself.

How it fits together

Why pictures get stuck and what each step does

The problem. OnBuy fetches a product's pictures from the links we send. Its image workers sometimes time out; then the pictures are never applied and the product page stays "loading" or answers 404. The links are fine - OnBuy confirmed it is a fault on their side and has no date for a fix.

The ticket gives their support what they ask for: the OPC (OnBuy's product code) of each affected product with its picture links, so they can process the images by hand. We check every link first so they cannot answer "your link is dead".

The resubmission is the workaround OnBuy's agent gave: send the product again through the products endpoint; the image workers then get a new job. It is not a cure - new products are still affected - which is why the ticket stays open.

Each resubmission run and each OnBuy lookup reads all the store's listings, and OnBuy answers "too many requests" after the 4th or 5th within an hour: the page refuses to start one while {{ data.sweeps }} have started in the last hour and says how long to wait.
